LECTURE COMPANION · MISHIG

Feynman Interactive

A visual companion to chapters 1–14 of The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Volume I. Scroll-synced wireframe demonstrations connect the reading with moving, adjustable models. Its lecture-plus-visual approach helped inspire Vibe Physics AI.

Hugging Face Space created: 1 October 2026. Hosting metadata ↗
This is the Space creation date, not a verified original project release date.

INTERACTIVE SCIENCE · UNIVERSITY OF COLORADO BOULDER

PhET Interactive Simulations

Research-based science and mathematics simulations built around exploration. Change a setting, move an object or use a virtual measuring tool to investigate cause and effect. A useful companion for building intuition before working through calculations.

Founded: 2002, by Carl Wieman. Official history source ↗

Explore project ↗
MATH, PHYSICS & ENGINEERING · PAUL FALSTAD

Falstad Physics Applets

Visual demonstrations of waves, electric and magnetic fields, circuits, optics and quantum mechanics. Explore how changing a parameter changes the behavior. The collection began with Java applets, many of which have since been converted to JavaScript.

Collection creation date: not confirmed.
The official collection page does not state a single founding date; individual applet dates are not treated as the start of the whole collection.

Explore project ↗
MECHANICS & NUMERICAL MODELS · ERIK NEUMANN

myPhysicsLab

Interactive mechanics simulations covering springs, pendulums, collisions and rigid bodies. Drag objects, change parameters such as gravity and damping, and connect the motion with the mathematical model. Many simulations include explanations of the underlying equations.

Development started: 2001. Author’s account ↗
