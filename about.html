ABOUT VIBE PHYSICS AI

Physics, a little

A growing library that puts lectures beside interactive diagrams, guided explanations and practice. Read an idea, explore what changes, then check what you understand.

I’m a fourth-year BSc Physics student in the Department of Physics, College of Science, University of Sulaimani. I’m building this space to make physics more visual, approachable and useful for studying—one subject and one chapter at a time.

Independent. Open source. Built for learning.

Vibe Physics AI is my independent educational project, not an official university website or an endorsement by the university or lecturers. It pairs original lecture pages with interactive educational visualizations, guided explanations and practice.

Explore moving waves, compare ordinary light with laser light, rotate crystal structures, or change a control to see what happens. The diagrams mainly use Canvas and Three.js/WebGL, with playback controls where motion helps explain an idea. The layout adapts to phones and computers.

The original website code is open source under the MIT license. You can explore how it works, reuse and improve the code, report scientific mistakes and suggest improvements. Lecture PDFs and third-party materials are excluded and retain their own ownership and permissions. See the license scope for details.
